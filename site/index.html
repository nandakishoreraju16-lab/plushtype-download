Free voice typing · Windows and Mac

Talk.
It types.

Say “type” and speak: your words appear in whatever app you are using. It can also open apps, search the web and close windows by voice. No account, no subscription, and your voice never leaves your computer.

Install in three steps

Windows

  1. Download the file and open it.
  2. If Windows says “Windows protected your PC”, click More info, then Run anyway. This warning appears for every new app that is not signed with a paid certificate.
  3. Click Install. The first start downloads the speech files once (about 660 MB); after that it works offline.

Mac

  1. Download the file, open it and drag Plushtype into Applications.
  2. Open it. If macOS says it cannot check the app, click Done, then System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.
  3. Allow Microphone, Accessibility (so it can type for you) and Automation when asked. The first start downloads the speech files once (about 660 MB).

What you can say

“type” …

Types what you say into the app in front.

“stop”

Ends typing.

“send it” / “enter”

Presses Enter.

“open Notepad”

Opens it. Add “in the background” to keep your screen.

“search funny cats”

Shows the results in Chrome.

“close Chrome”

Closes the app, or just the tab you name.

Private by design

The speech recognition runs on your own computer. Plushtype has no account and sends nothing anywhere: the only network use is the one-time download of the speech files. It listens all the time but types only after you say your trigger word, and a small capsule on the screen always shows what it is doing.
